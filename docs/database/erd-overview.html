Platform Data Model

The PostgreSQL design behind a multi-agency disaster-response platform for Government, Police, Ambulance and Fire services. One shared incident and one georeferenced map. Every AI result can be traced to the model and image that produced it.

What the data serves

Six features

F1

Before/After Damage & Restoration Cost

Each building footprint gets an xBD damage grade (No Damage, Minor, Major, Destroyed) with a probability per grade. Indicative restoration cost = HAZUS repair ratio × floor area × local cost per m², shown as a low–high range with editable assumptions.

buildingsbuilding_assessmentscost_estimates
F2

2D/3D Mapping & Rescue Routing

Stitched orthomosaic plus a 3D city view share the same layers. Blocked, flooded and debris-covered roads change edge costs. pgRouting returns the safest fastest route for the specific vehicle, plus alternatives.

raster_productsroad_segmentsroutes
F3

Aerial Person Detection, Partial Visibility

Finds people from any drone altitude or angle, also when only head or limbs show. Every detection stores visibility level, occlusion ratio, posture, GPS position and an uncertainty radius in metres.

person_detectionsimages
F4

Multi-Object Person Tracking

Detections are linked into tracks with a stable ID, path, last-known position and movement state. A stationary person may be injured. Tracks become assistance cases that a team can be sent to.

person_tracksassistance_casescase_events
F5

Compound Hazard Alerts

A rule engine watches how hazards interact in space. Electrified Water: floodwater within 30 m of an energized power line opens a no-entry zone, closes roads, re-plans routes and warns responders.

F6

General Reports

Situation, damage and cost, casualty, mission after-action and full-incident reports. Statistics are frozen at generation. Reports go through approval and can be shared between agencies.

Agency + Role

Who uses it

Users belong to one agency (Government, Police, EMS or Fire) and hold one of four roles that are the same in every agency. The Platform Admin sits above all agencies and is the only user without one. A CHECK constraint enforces that rule.

RoleScopeMain permissions
PLATFORM_ADMINWhole systemCreate agencies, manage agency admins, model registry, global hazard rules
AGENCY_ADMINOwn agencyUsers, teams and vehicles of the agency
COMMANDERJoined incidentsCreate incidents and missions, invite agencies, assign teams, approve reports, resolve alerts
ANALYSTJoined incidentsUpload imagery, run analysis, review AI results, plan routes, verify citizen requests
FIELD_RESPONDERAssigned missionsMobile app: tasks, live GPS, SOS, field reports, case status

Identity & Access

  • Agency and role are two independent axes. Permissions are rows in role_permissions, editable without code.
  • users CHECK: role is PLATFORM_ADMIN exactly when agency_id is null.
  • One active team per responder (partial unique index on team_members.user_id WHERE left_at IS NULL). This follows the ICS unity-of-command rule.
  • vehicles.max_water_depth_cm feeds routing: a 4x4 may cross shallow water, an ambulance may not.

Imagery & AI Pipeline

  • The provenance backbone. Every AI output row carries inference_run_id, which points to the model version, job and input rasters.
  • capture_sessions.phase (PRE_EVENT or POST_EVENT) is the before/after axis of F1, so pre-event satellite plus post-event drone imagery also works.

  • gsd_cm and gps_accuracy_m drive the uncertainty radius, and they block person detection on images taken from too high.

Buildings, Damage & Cost

  • area_m2 is a generated column: ST_Area(footprint::geography).
  • Assessments keep full history. A partial unique index allows exactly one is_current verdict per building.
  • Each verdict comes from AI or from a person: CHECK (inference_run_id IS NOT NULL OR assessed_by IS NOT NULL). A human review_status confirms or corrects it.
  • Cost = floor area × unit rate × repair ratio, from editable cost-profile tables. cost_estimates are snapshots, so a published report never changes silently.

Feature F2 · 5 tables

Road Network & Routing

  • road_segments follows the pgRouting edge contract: id, source, target, cost, reverse_cost. A negative cost means closed, so closing a road is one UPDATE.
  • Btree indexes on source and target, GiST on geom.
  • Current condition is cached on the segment. road_condition_observations keeps who reported it: AI, a responder or a citizen (exactly one).
  • route_segments lets the system find every active route through a closed road with one indexed join.

Persons, Tracks & Assistance Cases

  • Three levels: raw AI detection, then a track (the same person across frames), then an assistance case (a person a human decided needs help).
  • visibility and occlusion_ratio record partial sightings (head only, limbs only). The scope is visible persons only, never persons under rubble.
  • AI tracks, citizen requests and responder reports all end up in assistance_cases, triaged with START tags: red, yellow, green, black.
  • person_detections is high volume, with a BRIN index on detected_at and GiST on location.

Electrified Water

Floodwater within 30 m of an energized power line or transformer. One spatial query finds it.

SELECT h.id AS hazard_id, a.id AS asset_id
FROM hazards h
JOIN infrastructure_assets a
  ON a.incident_id = h.incident_id
 AND ST_DWithin(h.geom::geography, a.geom::geography, 30)
WHERE h.hazard_type = 'FLOOD_WATER'
  AND h.status IN ('SUSPECTED', 'CONFIRMED')
  AND a.asset_type IN ('POWER_LINE', 'TRANSFORMER')
  AND a.power_state <> 'DE_ENERGIZED';

Then, in one transaction

  1. Insert the compound_alert with a buffered danger_zone, and its compound_alert_sources.
  2. Close intersecting roads: record them in alert_closed_segments and set cost = reverse_cost = -1.
  3. Mark active routes through those segments INVALIDATED, found via route_segments, and queue re-planning.
  4. Notify assigned teams and every responder whose last position is inside the zone.

Field, Citizens, Reports & Audit

  • responder_positions is partitioned by month, with a BRIN index on time. The live map reads only responder_last_positions (one row per user, upserted).
  • Citizens sign in by phone OTP and store minimal personal data. Requests are auto-matched to an incident with ST_Contains(incidents.area, location), de-duplicated, then verified by an analyst.
  • Reports freeze their numbers in stats_snapshot and go DRAFT → APPROVED → PUBLISHED.
  • Similar past cases: vector(768) DINOv2 embeddings with an HNSW cosine index.

Applied everywhere

Conventions

ConcernMechanism
KeysUUID v7 for business entities, which the offline mobile app can generate. bigint for high-volume tables and the pgRouting graph.
SpatialSRID 4326 everywhere. GiST on every geometry. Metres via ST_DWithin(a::geography, b::geography, m), never ST_Distance < x.
VolumeMonthly partitions plus BRIN for GPS and audit. Cache tables for live views.
Provenanceinference_run_id + confidence on every AI row. A human review_status or verified_by alongside.
Exactly one sourceCHECK (num_nonnulls(...) = 1) on observations, hazards, assignments and alert sources.
Current + historyFast current-state columns, plus append-only history tables for the full timeline.
VocabulariesPostgres enums for fixed statuses. Lookup tables for anything an agency may edit: roles, rules, cost profiles.
AccessAn agency sees an incident only with an ACTIVE incident_agencies row. Enforced in the API, optionally with Row-Level Security.
FilesMinIO object storage. Postgres keeps storage_key, sha256 and metadata.
